  HOW TO ADD IMAGES (Simple 4-Step Process)

Step 1: GET FOOD IMAGES
  ├─ Download from stock photo sites (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay)
  ├─ Take photos of your actual dishes
  └─ Or use sample images temporarily

Step 2: SAVE TO CORRECT FOLDER
  Location: static\images\menu\
  
  Example:
    static\images\menu\roast.jpg
    static\images\menu\butter-chicken.jpg
    static\images\menu\dal-makhani.jpg

Step 3: UPDATE VIA ADMIN PANEL
  1. Login: http://localhost:5000
  2. Email: admin@foodsystem.com
  3. Password: admin123
  4. Go to: Menu Management
  5. Click: "Edit" on menu item
  6. Image field: Enter filename (e.g., "roast.jpg")
  7. Click: "Update Menu Item"

Step 4: VERIFY
  • Go to Menu List page
  • Your item now shows the actual food image!
  • No more generic placeholders! ✅
